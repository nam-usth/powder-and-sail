# Perf: GFX HIGH vs LOW, 2026-11-05 (art pass 2)

## Method
- Headless Chromium through `tools/smoke.mjs`, **SwiftShader software GL** on 4 shared CPU cores. Other agents were using the machine at the same time (load average 8 to 17).
- Viewport 1280×720 at DPR 1. The auto-resolution governor is off on software GL.
- Render-only cost, measured synchronously in one `page.evaluate` so the game's rAF loop cannot interleave:
  - Per frame: the planar reflection (HIGH only), then `Renderer.render()`, then a 1-pixel `gl.readPixels` to force the GPU work to finish.
  - Two warm-up frames, then the median of 8 frames.
  - Runs alternate LOW, HIGH, LOW, HIGH.
  - `renderer.info` is reset at the start of each frame.
- Script: `bench.mjs` (scratchpad). You can reproduce it with `MISSION=<id> node tools/smoke.mjs --port <p> --path "/frontend/?mode=harbor" --script bench.mjs`.
- Absolute ms are software-raster numbers and are only useful for comparing HIGH with LOW. A real GPU is far faster.

## Results (median ms per frame, after the art pass)

| Scene | Tier | median ms | draw calls | triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harbor (default camera, town on) | LOW | 130–135 | 76 | 85.7 k |
| Harbor | HIGH | 892–997 | 135 | 252.5 k |
| Battle `storm_3` (Chinese citadel, 3 islands, junks + carracks) | LOW | 81–84 | 68–72 | 52–56 k |
| Battle `storm_3` | HIGH | 303–565 | 122–124 | 173 k |

The cost before the art pass was measured on 2026-11-04 with the same method on a less loaded machine. Harbor: LOW 272–284 ms (76 calls, 87 k tris) and HIGH 1665–1800 ms (134 calls, 255 k tris).

**LOW is about 6–7× cheaper than HIGH in the harbor and about 4–7× cheaper in battle. Both are well above the 2× requirement.** On HIGH the time goes to the 4x MSAA float target, the shadow map pass, the planar reflection and bloom.

## What the art pass costs
- **Harbor town** (about 30 houses, sheds and warehouses, 4 quay stalls and a market, crates and barrels, steps):
  - Baked into the existing harbor ModelKit meshes, so it adds **0 draw calls**.
  - Triangle counts stayed within run noise of the earlier numbers.
  - LOW drops every `hi` part: windows, chimneys, balconies, clutter and goods.
- **Chimney smoke**: one `THREE.Points` draw (+1 call on HIGH) of about 77 points, animated entirely in the vertex shader, with no CPU work per frame. It is hidden on LOW.
- **Ship liveries**: sail shader change only (wider foot band and a head band). The bigger flags and pennants add a few vertices per ship. No new draws.
- **Fort scale and Asian vegetation**: these use instance and transform changes only. The same instanced meshes are used.

## HIGH visual check
I looked at HIGH screenshots of the harbor and of battles at `reef_2`, `storm_1`, `storm_3`, `spice_1`, `spice_3`, `indian_3` and `straits_1`.
- No shadow acne showed on terrain, houses or forts. Contact shadows and palm shadows on the shallows look plausible.
- Bloom is not blowing out. The sun glint on the sea is bright but stays in range.
- The cloud wisps at harbor close zoom sit over the bottom-left ships. This is the intended foreground-wisp effect and was left as it is.
- The east and west harbor shots that looked "blue" turned out to be the day cycle reaching dusk, not an artifact. The QA shots now pin the time of day.
- Gap found and fixed: battle islands always used the Caribbean biome and the castillo fort for unclaimed islands. Each island now takes its sea from `def.region`.
- Gap found and fixed: forts were toy-sized on r 10–14 islands. They are now scaled ×1–1.55 with the island radius.
