⚓ ISLES OF WAR
1 — Command your fleet
Drag a box (or click a ship) to select. Right-click the sea to sail, an enemy ship to attack, an island to besiege it. Shift+right-click queues waypoints; Space centres the camera.
2 — Fleets, formations & stances
Ctrl+1–9 saves the selection as a fleet, 1–9 recalls it. F cycles Line Ahead / Line Abreast / Wedge; T cycles Aggressive / Defensive / Hold Fire. Ships fight broadside-on: they circle a target keeping their strongest side toward it.
3 — Capture, build & research
Right-click a neutral island (or an enemy one whose fort is down) to capture it.
B opens the build dock on your island; R opens Research.
4 — Push the War Front
Capturing islands, sinking ships and out-producing the enemy push the front bar (top). Reach 100% to win; at 0% you lose.
5 — Market & Fog
Press M to open the Market — trade wood for gold.
Press Shift+F to toggle Fog of War. ? shows every control.
